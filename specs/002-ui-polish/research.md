# Research: UI Polish of the Session Flow

**Feature**: `002-ui-polish` | **Date**: 2026-11-04 | **Plan**: [plan.md](plan.md)

Each entry: Decision, Rationale, Alternatives considered. The apple-design skill
(`.claude/skills/apple-design/SKILL.md`) is written for the web; every decision maps it to the
native iPad equivalents. Detail lives in the three contracts.

## Rendering: the lamp moment ([contracts/lamp-transition.md](contracts/lamp-transition.md))

### D1. Interruptible lamp integrator

- **Decision**: `uvAmount` follows a rate-limited ramp (0.7 s) smoothed by a critically damped
  spring (response 0.2 s = `Motion.quick`). A switch from rest starts with velocity 1/0.7 per
  second. Reversals keep position and velocity.
- **Rationale**: No jump and no "brick wall" on reversal (FR-004, skill §3); the rate limit gives a
  hard per-frame brightness bound; the starting velocity makes the first frame change visibly, so
  the click can land on it. Simulated at 24/30/60 fps with random reversals and dropped frames
  (simulation only; not yet on a device).
- **Alternatives**: a bare spring (too fast at the start, fails SC-001 at 30 fps once channels are
  eased); a fixed curve run backwards (flips speed in one frame).

### D2. One variable drives every channel, in order

- **Decision**: room darkening `h(a/0.78)` with `h(x)=x+x²−x³`; skin `smoothstep(0.05, 0.78, a)`;
  glow gate `smoothstep(0.80, 1, a)`; mixed in the camera's encoded colour space.
- **Rationale**: skin before glow on switch-on and glow out before skin on switch-off (FR-002) by
  construction; simulated worst case (all-white → black) ≤ 0.085 per frame at 30 fps, ≤ 0.093
  with random reversals; typical scenes ≤ 0.04 (SC-001 limit 0.10).
- **Alternatives**: a separate glow stage after `uvAmount` = 1 (switch-off ≈ 1.05 s, outside
  FR-001).

### D3. Glow spread by anatomical order

- **Decision**: each mark gets a `revealOrder` from its anchor's joint-chain distance (tip 0, DIP
  1/3, PIP 2/3, MCP 1, wrist 2, forearm 2.25, normalized); reveal factor
  `smoothstep((glowReveal·1.35 − revealOrder)/0.35)`, forced to 1 at `glowReveal` = 1. The spread
  starts at `uvAmount ≥ 0.99`, once per session.
- **Rationale**: computed once, independent of pose; final frame identical to the all-at-once
  reveal; each mark fades in over ≈ 0.39 s, which reads as a spread, not a scanner.
- **Alternatives**: projecting marks on a screen axis each frame (order changes with pose); a
  narrow window (scanner look).

### D4. Badge leads glow, with an interlock

- **Decision**: the renderer publishes `badgeRequired = uvTarget == 1 || uvAmount > 0` in the same
  call as the switch; the glow is drawn only once the UI reports the badge fully opaque. The UI
  also shows the badge from uvIntro entry (D10).
- **Rationale**: the badge leads the first glow by ≥ 0.6 s and stays ≥ 0.7 s after it; the
  interlock makes SC-002 hold by construction, including after a face-guard resume.
- **Alternatives**: 001's `glowVisible` (trails the glow by a frame, violates FR-003).

### D5. Lamp click timing and asset

- **Decision**: AppModel plays `LampClick` on every animated switch-on, in the same turn as the
  switch, never delayed; skipped if a clip started < 0.25 s earlier; the next clip start waits
  0.8 s. Asset: a bundled 110 ms uncompressed CAF (two soft clicks, −18 dBFS) generated by
  `tools/make_lamp_click.py`, on the narration's audio session.
- **Rationale**: harmony (same frame) and causality (only at switch-on) from skill §13; the click
  never covers a clip's first words; in the first reveal N05 starts with the first glow.
- **Alternatives**: delaying the click (breaks sync); a system sound (cannot be frame-timed or
  reviewed by partner staff).

## Motion and surfaces ([contracts/motion-and-surfaces.md](contracts/motion-and-surfaces.md))

### D6. Prompts appear in place; only hints move

- **Decision**: `promptTransition` is opacity + scale 0.92 → 1 in place, exit the exact reverse;
  opacity only with Reduce Motion. Direction comes from hints (arrow nudge, turn-hand flip) that
  move once per narration cue.
- **Rationale**: if entries never travel, any movement means "go this way" (skill §7, §8, FR-008).
- **Alternatives**: a small move in the pointing direction (mixes arrival with guidance motion).

### D7. Reduced lamp is a 0.4 s linear cross-fade

- **Decision**: with Reduce Motion, the lamp is one 0.4 s linear cross-fade, then the glow fades in
  over 0.2 s (total 0.6 s, no spread).
- **Rationale**: a 0.2 s fade would reach ≈ 28% per frame at 30 fps and fail SC-001; 0.6 s total is
  still shorter than the standard change (US1 #5).
- **Alternatives**: `Motion.reducedFade` for the lamp too (fails SC-001 on bright scenes).

### D8. Instant press highlight; spring only on scale

- **Decision**: `PressableStyle` (a `ButtonStyle`) shows its highlight with no animation on
  touch-down; the 0.96 scale uses `Motion.quick`; no scale with Reduce Motion.
- **Rationale**: a 0.2 s spring moves only ~10% in the first frame, too little for SC-003; a
  `ButtonStyle` keeps the system's act-on-release, drag-off cancel, and VoiceOver.
- **Alternatives**: `PrimitiveButtonStyle` with hand-written touch tracking.

### D9. Step indicator: 8 steps, rehearsal as one step with six sub-ticks

- **Decision**: positioning, normal view, lamp, reveal, quick rinse, rehearsal, real wash, debrief;
  rehearsal shows six small segments inside its capsule.
- **Rationale**: 13 dots cannot be counted at a glance (SC-004); a capsule jumping every 4–5 s
  would pull the participant's eye to the strip (FR-013).
- **Alternatives**: six top-level rehearsal steps; one step without sub-ticks.

### D10. Glass only on the facilitator strip, with an instant fixed-order fallback

- **Decision**: the facilitator strip uses dark `.thinMaterial` + black 0.3 tint + 1 pt top
  highlight (the simulation badge is always a solid chip, D17); solid when Increase Contrast, then Reduce Transparency, then quality
  `.medium`/`.low`; switching is instant. Panels (consent, camera error, rehearsal demo) and calm
  screens are solid.
- **Rationale**: legibility over a moving camera image (skill §12) at the lowest GPU cost; no
  blur animation over live video.
- **Alternatives**: the skill's blur-ramp "materialize" effect; glass panels.

## Choreography ([contracts/choreography.md](contracts/choreography.md))

### D11. Badge from uvIntro, not from glow

- **Decision**: the badge appears when N04 starts, is opaque within 0.2 s, and stays through
  normalView2 until a calm surface covers it.
- **Rationale**: FR-003 holds by seconds, not frames; no flicker on fast toggles.
- **Alternatives**: tying it to `glowVisible`; hiding it in normalView2 (races the glow).

### D12. No clip within 0.8 s after the click

- **Decision**: N05 starts when `uvAmount` reaches 1, together with the first glow, also with
  Reduce Motion on.
- **Rationale**: FR-016; N05 begins "Look", so the word lands on the first glow; narration timing
  never depends on accessibility settings (with Reduce Motion the glow is already in at t0 + 0.6 s,
  and N05 still starts at t0 + 0.8 s).
- **Alternatives**: N05 at once (overlaps the click); after the spread (1.5 s of silence).

### D13. Turn-hand prompt flips once

- **Decision**: a hand glyph that turns over 180° about the forearm axis once per N06, replacing
  001's circular arrows; a mirrored cross-fade with Reduce Motion.
- **Rationale**: turning over is the movement asked for; circular arrows say "spin". Needs partner
  sign-off (SC-008).
- **Alternatives**: spinning circular arrows; a looping animation.

### D14. Orientation

- **Decision**: participant at the bottom edge, strip at the top, in both setups; the positioning
  arrow sits above the hand outline and nudges onto it; the glow spread follows each hand's own
  fingers, not the screen.
- **Rationale**: in setup A screen-down is toward her body, so a downward hint must end on a
  target; anatomical spread is correct at any hand angle.
- **Alternatives**: an arrow rising from her edge. To check in the pilot.

### D15. Calm screens cross-fade from the last camera frame

- **Decision**: on debrief, end, or Stop, the session ends (or advances) at once, capture stops,
  and the renderer stops presenting, so the view keeps its last frame while the solid calm surface
  fades in over it (smoothstep, 0.8 s, the same for Stop). The badge and Stop stay above/under it
  per choreography §7; the lamp is not snapped until the surface is opaque; face guard stays an
  instant blank (FR-020, FR-024).
- **Rationale**: 001 cut straight to black (an abrupt brightness jump, skill §14).
- **Alternatives**: blanking at once (today); fading the camera to black first (two steps).

### D16. The first reveal needs the spoken explanation

- **Decision**: the lamp switches on for the first time only after N04 has played to its end and
  uvIntro has lasted ≥ 1.0 s. A missing or failing N04 makes the lamp screen wait for the
  facilitator's Next (after an in-person explanation). The demo launcher, and later 001's Device
  Check, refuse to start a session if any of N01–N21 is missing.
- **Rationale**: Principle I requires the first reveal to include a spoken explanation; a timer
  fallback would show the glow unexplained (analysis C2).
- **Alternatives**: a 1.0 s timer fallback (the previous design).

### D17. One accessibility profile for every study iPad; a solid badge

- **Decision**: sessions run with Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency, and Increase Contrast off and
  the default text size. `StudySettings` reads them and the iPadOS version at session start, warns
  the facilitator if they differ, and records them (in 001's `deviceCheck` event once logging
  exists). The simulation badge is always a solid chip.
- **Rationale**: Principle III: the intervention must be the same for every participant; the
  accessibility variants still exist for robustness and for a recorded, deliberate exception
  (analysis C5).
- **Alternatives**: letting each iPad's settings decide (participants would see different
  reveals); disabling accessibility support (fails FR-017 to FR-019).

### D18. Recording protocol for validation

- **Decision**: screen recordings of the camera view (Q1–Q15) are made only with consenting
  non-cohort volunteers, on a QA iPad with iCloud Photos off and Airplane Mode on, moved by cable,
  deleted after measuring with the deletion logged; the face-guard check uses a printed face of a
  consenting team member.
- **Rationale**: Principle II: the app never stores frames, so the validation process must not
  create an uncontrolled copy of hands or faces (analysis C6).
- **Alternatives**: measuring on device only (no repeatable brightness numbers).

## Changes required in 001 code (found by the contracts)

1. N05 must start 0.8 s after uvReveal entry, not on the same frame (D12).
2. The badge must not depend on `glowVisible` alone (D4, D11).
3. uvIntro must not switch the lamp on before N04 has played to its end and ≥ 1.0 s have passed;
   a missing N04 waits for the facilitator (D16).
4. Debrief/abort must not blank the screen at once, and Stop must stay on the debrief (D15,
   FR-007).
5. Clip-start cues must be latched in `AppModel`, not view state, so pauses and missing clips
   cannot lose them; pauses and face guard freeze `uvAmount`, `glowReveal`, and prompt fallback
   timers, and resuming never replays the click, the spread, or prompt entrances.
6. `AppModel` must expose the state machine's `resumeState`, so the step indicator keeps its place
   while paused.

## Reconciliation (2026-11-04)

The three contracts were drafted in parallel. Conflicts were settled as: lamp integrator and
reversal rule from lamp-transition §2; glow returns through the glow gate on toggles (inside the
0.8 s change); reduced lamp 0.4 s linear (D7); hold-to-end ring empties with `Motion.standard`;
panels solid (D10); system drag-off distance (D8).
