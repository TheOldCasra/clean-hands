# Back-translation (to be filled by an independent translator)

A second Swahili speaker, who has **not** seen the English source, translates each reviewed
Swahili line from [script.sw.md](script.sw.md) back into English. The project team then compares
it with the English source and resolves every difference in meaning with the first translator
(tasks T094). Nobody on the app team fills this in: a back-translation by the draft's author
checks nothing.

Flag especially: anything that could sound like the glow is a real test (Principle I), any
shaming wording (Principle V), and anything about prayer or wudu (Principle VII).
